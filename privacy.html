Maxfiylik siyosati (Dialab CRM)

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 31-oktabr.

1. Biz kimmiz

Dialab CRM, Dialab mijozlari bilan ishlash uchun ichki dastur. Unga faqat Dialab xodimlari kiradi. Aloqa: neosocialtarget@gmail.com

2. Qanday ma'lumot yig'amiz

  • Instagram va Facebook orqali bizga yozgan yoki reklama formasini to'ldirgan odamning ismi, foydalanuvchi ID'si, yozgan xabarlari va (formada ko'rsatilgan bo'lsa) telefon raqami.
  • Xodim kiritgan ma'lumotlar: telefon, murojaat holati, to'lov holati, izohlar.
  • Bizning Instagram va Facebook sahifamizning umumiy statistikasi (obunachilar, qamrov).

3. Ma'lumotdan nima uchun foydalanamiz

Faqat mijozning savoliga javob berish, murojaatni yuritish, to'lovni kuzatish va xizmat sifatini yaxshilash uchun. Ma'lumot sotilmaydi va reklama maqsadida uchinchi tomonlarga berilmaydi.

4. Ma'lumot qayerda saqlanadi

Ma'lumotlar Dialab egasining o'z serverida saqlanadi. Kirish tokenlari va parollar faqat serverda saqlanadi va foydalanuvchilarga ko'rsatilmaydi. Ma'lumotlarga faqat vakolatli xodimlar kiradi.

5. Saqlash muddati va o'chirish

Ma'lumot xizmat ko'rsatish uchun kerak bo'lgan muddatgacha saqlanadi. O'chirishni so'rash tartibi ma'lumotni o'chirish sahifasida yozilgan.

6. Sizning huquqlaringiz

Siz o'zingiz haqingizdagi ma'lumotni so'rashingiz, to'g'rilashingiz yoki o'chirishni talab qilishingiz mumkin. Buning uchun neosocialtarget@gmail.com manziliga yozing.

7. O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, shu sahifa yangilanadi.

Privacy Policy (Dialab CRM)

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

1. Who we are

Dialab CRM is an internal tool used by Dialab to manage its customers and inquiries. Only Dialab staff can sign in. Contact: neosocialtarget@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

  • When a person messages us on Instagram or Facebook, or submits one of our ad forms: their name, their platform user ID, the messages they send us and, if provided in the form, their phone number.
  • Data entered by staff: phone number, inquiry status, payment status, notes.
  • Aggregate statistics of our own Instagram and Facebook pages (followers, reach).

3. How we use it

Only to reply to customers, manage inquiries, track payments and improve our service. We do not sell the data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

4. Where it is stored

Data is stored on the Dialab owner's own server. Access tokens and passwords stay on the server and are never shown to users. Only authorised staff can access the data.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as it is needed to provide the service. See how to request deletion.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you by emailing neosocialtarget@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
