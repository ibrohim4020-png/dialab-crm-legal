Ma'lumotni o'chirish ko'rsatmasi

Instagram yoki Facebook orqali bizga yozgan bo'lsangiz va ma'lumotingiz o'chirilishini xohlasangiz:

  1. neosocialtarget@gmail.com manziliga xat yozing. Mavzu: «Ma'lumotni o'chirish».
  2. Xatda Instagram yoki Facebook'dagi nomingizni (foydalanuvchi nomini) yozing.
  3. Biz 7 kun ichida ma'lumotingizni (ism, xabarlar, telefon raqami) o'chirib, sizga xabar beramiz.

Shuningdek, ilovaga bergan ruxsatni Facebook sozlamalaridan ham olib tashlashingiz mumkin: Sozlamalar > Ilovalar va veb-saytlar.

Batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati.

Data deletion instructions

If you contacted us via Instagram or Facebook and want your data deleted:

  1. Email neosocialtarget@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion".
  2. Include your Instagram or Facebook name (username).
  3. Within 7 days we will delete your data (name, messages, phone number) and confirm by reply.

You can also remove the app's access in your Facebook settings: Settings > Apps and Websites.

More details: Privacy Policy.
